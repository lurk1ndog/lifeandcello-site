Music at the bedside

Music… You language where languages end.Rainer Maria Rilke, “An die Musik”

End of life bedside performances

As an End-of-Life Cellist, I approach my work with utmost compassion and empathy. I understand the significance of caring for individuals who are leaving their bodies as well as for their loved ones who are alongside them. I began this work with friends and family and it has evolved into a passion project. I have taken in-home funeral training to get acquainted with bringing aspects of dying and death into the home.

Research indicates that playing music when someone is dying can help alleviate anxiety, access emotions, and facilitate a mode of communication that transcends the need for verbal expression. I recognize the transformative power of music during these times and treat each encounter with kindness and respect, ensuring that these profound moments are filled with comfort and compassion.

I work with each family to customize the experience. Please contact me to learn more.

End of Life Music Project

I’ve been in correspondence with Kinan Azmeh since just after I graduated from the San Francisco Conservatory of Music. During my time there I learned and played one of his pieces. He is a Syrian composer and clarinetist who, among many other things, has written works for Yo-Yo Ma and won a Grammy Award with Yo-Yo Ma’s Silk Road Ensemble.

We have been talking about my work playing cello bedside for people in hospice, and he became ‘tremendously’ interested in collaborating with me on a solo cello suite for this type of work. Just recently, he began work on a suite for solo cello that is going to be music specifically for this situation.

In a recent phone conversation with Mr. Azmeh, he told me: ‘Your project has so much connection with life. It is a very simple concept that nobody thinks about. It is such an obvious... it is moving. To think that someone can do something for somebody in that situation.’

I am now reaching out to other composers who create authentically and are deeply connected with the natural world and humanity through their work. I want to create a body of compositions that will serve to comfort people and families as they navigate death and the transition of dying.

Please reach out to me if you would like to learn more about this project, and check out the GoFundMe page.
